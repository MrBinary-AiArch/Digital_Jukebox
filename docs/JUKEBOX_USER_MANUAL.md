# Digital Jukebox - User Guide

## 1. How to Add a New CD
1.  **Insert the CD** into the disc drive on the front of the silver HP tower.
2.  **Wait.** The machine will automatically start reading the music.
    *   *Note: This usually takes about 5 to 10 minutes.*
3.  **Finished!** When the light stops flashing and the job is done, the **disc tray will pop open automatically**.
4.  **Remove the CD** and close the tray. You can now insert the next one.

---

## 2. How to See What Is Happening (Optional)
If you want to see how much time is left on a disc:

1.  Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari).
2.  Type this address into the top bar:
    **http://digitaljukebox.local:8080**
3.  You will see a status bar showing the progress.

---

## 3. How to Listen to Your Music
1.  Open your web browser.
2.  Type this address into the top bar:
    **http://digitaljukebox.local:32400/web**
3.  Click on **Music** on the left side.
4.  Your new album will appear there automatically. 
    *   *If you don't see it immediately, wait 1-2 minutes for the system to organize the pictures.*

---

### **Troubleshooting**
*   **Disc didn't eject?** Press the small button on the drive. If it was a movie or a damaged disc, the system might have rejected it.
*   **System off?** Press the power button on the front. Wait 2 minutes before trying to use it.
